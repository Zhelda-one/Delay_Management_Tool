Contact/Support
===============

If you're experiencing issues with BBA or have a suggestion for a new feature, feel free to contact us at: I_SOC_BB_ANALYZER_SUPPORT@INTERNAL.NSN.COM.

.. note::
    Please provide the following information in your email:
        - description of the problem;
        - attach the file;
        - screenshots;
        - steps to reproduce the problem;
        - any additional information that might help us to solve the problem.
